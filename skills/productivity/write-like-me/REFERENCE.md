# Write like me — reference

## Phrases and patterns to avoid (non-exhaustive)

- “It’s important to note”, “In today’s fast-paced world”  
- “Leverage”, “robust”, “seamless”, “unlock”, “transform”, “delve”, “tapestry”, “comprehensive”, “game-changer”  
- Hollow enthusiasm, symmetric polish, “I hope this message finds you well” as filler  

Prefer plain verbs and concrete nouns.

## Context tuning

### Slack / Teams

Short, direct; avoid wall-of-text; casual but still professional.

### Email

Clear opener, straight body, practical close—warm without stiffness.

### Documentation

Structured and clear; no marketing voice.

### Personal message

Warmer, conversational.

### Technical explanation

Keep precision; don’t oversimplify facts.

## Examples

### 1

**Input:** I wanted to reach out to provide an update regarding the current status of the project. We have made significant progress and are continuing to work through the remaining items to ensure everything is completed successfully.

**Output:** Quick update on the project. We’ve made decent progress and are working through the remaining bits now.

### 2

**Input:** This implementation enables a robust and scalable foundation for future enhancements while maintaining a clean separation of concerns.

**Output:** This gives us a decent foundation to build on without mixing everything together.

### 3

**Input:** It is important to note that while this approach may provide benefits, there are also potential trade-offs that should be considered.

**Output:** This approach has benefits, but there are trade-offs we need to be honest about.

### 4

**Input:** I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused and appreciate your patience as we work to resolve this matter.

**Output:** Sorry for the hassle. Thanks for bearing with us while we sort it.

### 5

**Input:** This feature will empower users to seamlessly manage their workflows in a more efficient and intuitive way.

**Output:** This should make it easier for users to manage the workflow without jumping between tools.

## Pattern reminder

**Bad:** This solution provides a comprehensive and robust way to streamline your workflow while ensuring scalability and maintainability.

**Better:** This gives us a cleaner way to manage the workflow without turning it into a mess later.
